🎵  MUSIC FOLDER
===============

Put your song in this folder and name it exactly:

    romantic-song.mp3

That's it. The music button (top-right corner) will play it.

• Music is OFF by default. It only starts when she taps the button,
  because browsers block music that starts on its own.
• It fades in gently and loops.
• If the file is missing, tapping the button shows a small note
  instead of doing nothing.

Want a different filename? Change CONFIG.music at the top of script.js,
e.g.   music: 'music/our-song.mp3',

Only use a song you own or have the right to use.
